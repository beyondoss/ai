# Catalog truth check: `MODEL_ROUTES` vs primary-vendor docs (2026-09-30)

Scope: every row in `crates/providers/src/catalog.rs` whose **primary** candidate is Groq, Together, Fireworks, Mistral, xAI, DeepSeek, OpenAI or Anthropic. I skipped the OpenRouter-only rows (claude-opus-4-1, claude-sonnet-4, gpt-5.1-codex*, gpt-5.2-codex, gpt-5.6-*-pro, gpt-6-astra-pro, o3-pro).

Method: I downloaded each vendor page raw with curl and parsed it locally, so the numbers below are quoted from page text, not from an LLM summary. Fallback identity comes from `https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/models` (`canonical_slug` and `name`), fetched 2026-09-30.

Notation: "ours" is `price(input, output, cache_read, cache_write)` and `card(ctx, max_out)`. Δ is ours compared with the vendor. **LOW** means we bill less than the vendor charges us (we lose margin). **HIGH** means we bill the customer more than the vendor's list price.

Sources:

- Groq: https://console.groq.com/docs/models and https://console.groq.com/docs/prompt-caching. groq.com/pricing now redirects to the homepage and has no price table.
- Together: https://docs.together.ai/docs/serverless/models and https://www.together.ai/pricing
- Fireworks: https://docs.fireworks.ai/serverless/pricing and https://fireworks.ai/models/fireworks/{id}. The model pages embed `"serverless":true|false`.
- Mistral: https://docs.mistral.ai/models and https://docs.mistral.ai/models/{slug}. mistral.ai/pricing renders prices client-side, so the per-model docs pages were used instead.
- xAI: https://docs.x.ai/developers/pricing.md, https://docs.x.ai/developers/models/{id}.md and https://docs.x.ai/developers/migration/may-15-retirement.md
- DeepSeek: https://api-docs.deepseek.com/quick_start/pricing and https://api-docs.deepseek.com/updates
- OpenAI: https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/pricing.md, https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/{id}.md and https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/deprecations.md. openai.com/api/pricing returns 403.
- Anthropic: https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing.md, .../models/overview.md, .../model-deprecations.md and .../models/{m}/overview.md
